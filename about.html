About me

Life in Product

Healthcare Technology & Consumer Products

I've been in product management for 15+ years taking products from 0→1 to scale across healthcare technology, electronics, and consumer goods.

Currently a Senior Product Manager at athenahealth, I've scaled mobile applications to 65,000+ monthly active users, built interactive clinical widgets that streamlined RCM workflows, conceptualised and deployed AI-powered development and GTM workflows that compress delivery cycles from weeks to hours, and helped make mobile platforms accessible to users with visual disabilities.

Before healthcare, I spent a decade in consumer electronics working with major global brands. I've launched sleep products for global retailers like Costco, Amazon, and Walmart, and led digital customer acquisition strategies for a boutique marketing firm.

I write here about product strategy, AI, and technology, with a hint of philosophy—asking questions that don't have easy answers yet. If that sounds like your kind of conversation, let's talk.

Work

Innovation & Research

In building the future of intelligent healthcare, I lead AI experiments for how we build and deploy software. The focus for next 3 years is on truly improving efficiencies in clinical workflows leveraging AI meaningfully. athenaOne Mobile

Mobile Solutions - iOS & Android

We're putting care in clinicians' pockets. A mobile platform serving over 65,000 healthcare providers, enabling clinical decision support and efficiencies anywhere, anytime. When a physician reviews critical labs while out of office, replies to patient inquiries, updates medications between rounds, or acts on assigned tasks, they're using tools designed for flexibility, never compromising on accuracy, speed or security. Every second saved is a moment returned to patient care and reduced burnout.

Inclusive Design

Healthcare for everyone means tools for everyone. VoiceOver support for athenaOne iOS doesn't just meet compliance standards, it opens doors.
